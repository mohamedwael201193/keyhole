# Landing assets (web/public/landing)

The v2 landing (DESIGN_PLAN_V2) uses four pictures from this folder, plus the site's own key art in `web/public/heirloom/keys`.

Real key art is not generated: every key that stands for our NFT is `web/public/heirloom/keys/k03.webp` (Bronze), `k04.webp` (Silver),
`k02.webp` (Gold) and `k01.webp` (Legendary finish). The landing draws them over the hero art and on the FoilCards; nothing here replaces them.

## How the two new pictures were made

Both pictures were generated by the owner with Higgsfield. The prompts are unknown, so none is recorded here.

- `hero-arch.webp`: the source PNG with the three generated keys painted out (a horizontal fill of the plain wall and the pedestal tops, plus a
  light grain), then cropped to x 320..1199, y 0..590 and saved as WebP at quality 92. The real k03, k04 and k02 are placed on the pedestals by CSS from the
  picture's own pixel coordinates (see `components/landing/LandingHero.tsx`: Bronze stem at x 660, Silver at 817.5, Gold at 972, feet at y 418, 390 and 418
  in the source), so they cover the spot at every width. The brass plaques under the pedestals are CSS, not part of the picture.
- `problem-keyhole.webp`: the source PNG cropped to y 62..662, resized to 1800 px wide, WebP quality 90. The keyhole centre sits at 23.1% across and 53% down
  the crop; the warm glow in `components/landing/Problem.tsx` is placed from those numbers.
